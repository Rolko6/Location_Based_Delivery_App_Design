## Design Specificity Verdict

LLM assessment: unmistakably Shoot. Range ring, "Location shared until delivery", "Café staff · has your photo", BeReal-style camera and "I'm waving" quick replies make no sense elsewhere. Strongest part: tracking (4.2 → 4.3) built on Mikko's "exact, not vague" and Laura's "find me". Weakest: what happens when the coffee arrives; the app goes silent.

Deterministic scan: not applicable (native platform).

## Overall Impression

Cleaner and more consistent than the first critique. The biggest gaps are no longer visual but what personas need and don't get: Laura never sees the location-sharing promise kept, Mikko has no fast repeat order, Aino has no group order. Privacy (C8) is the only one that is a promise already made on screen.

## What's Working

- Laura sees privacy everywhere it arises: 2.3, camera note, 2.3b ("…only until delivery"), map tag on 4.2.
- Mikko gets an exact time from menu to delivery, consistent across screens (9:51); no calls, only chat with quick replies.
- E1–E10 always say what happened and what next ("You haven't been charged", "your cart is saved", walking direction on the map).

## Priority Issues

- **[P1] "Until delivery" is never fulfilled on screen; the order has no end.** No Delivered state of 4.2; C8 (Laura) is the one story the app promises and doesn't show; peak-end has no end. Fix: 4.2 Delivered state: full olive progress, "Delivered at 9:51", map replaced by olive banner "Location sharing stopped", a line on what happens to the photo (needs a policy decision), chat closed. Command: /impeccable shape.
- **[P2] Example photos teach users to photograph strangers.** 2.3 example, 2.4c tips, E2 frame and the back camera show a storefront with five identifiable people; Laura at a playground would photograph other children. Fix: example of a spot without faces (bench/table + landmark, cropped from existing photos), tip "Show your spot, not other people"; product question: auto-blur strangers' faces before sending. Command: /impeccable clarify + asset swap.
- **[P2] After payment, what you ordered disappears.** 2.9 no longer has the receipt; 4.2 and 4.1 show only "3 items". Aino can't check her team's coffees on arrival; Mikko can't see what he paid. Fix: one row on 4.2 under the courier card "3 items · €11.70 ›" expanding to items and payment (not in the wireframe, needs approval). Command: /impeccable layout.
- **[P2] A repeat order is as long as the first.** Cart → 2.3 → selfie → spot → review → 2.7 → Face ID = 7 taps every time; no "Order again" on 1.6 or 2.10 earlier orders; the prototype still shows 2.3 every time (first-order-only rule is only in docs). Fix: prototype path 2.2 → 2.4 for repeat orders; consider "Same spot as before? Use this photo" within an hour; "Order again" once wireframed. Command: /impeccable shape.

## Persona Red Flags

- **Aino:** group order (C2, C3) not even hinted; can't see what she ordered on 4.2; the separate €0.50 fee is clear.
- **Mikko:** 7 taps from the cart, 2.3 every time, no "Order again"; exact time and no-call chat work well; can't see the amount after paying.
- **Laura:** back camera nudges her to photograph a space full of people (2.3 example); "until delivery" never changes state; back buttons top-left (2.2, 2.3, 2.7, 4.2); E9 "can't be refunded" reads harsh in a stressful moment.
- **Barista / Sanna:** photo quality is their only aid; tips are good but the example shows a crowd where nobody could be found.

## Minor Observations

- "Prepared" step label lights up while preparing ("Preparing" would read better; labels approved from the wireframe).
- 2.5 is the only checkout camera screen without the stepper.
- E5 background still shows the old "Scan your spot" title.
- "Oat milk" in the cart still can't be chosen in the menu.
- E8 shows the "[policy – TBD]" placeholder (intentional per brief).
- Dead links: "View all" (2.10), "Pick up at counter" (E8, E9), "Show me the way back" (E9).
- 4.3 quick reply "I moved – see my new photo" sends text instead of opening E10.

## Questions to Consider

- What does Laura see the second the coffee is in her hand, and what happens to her photo then?
- Can the last order's photo be reused if Mikko hasn't moved within an hour?
- Should the back camera blur strangers' faces before the photo goes to the café?
- Where does Aino's group order start: on the café page or in the cart?
