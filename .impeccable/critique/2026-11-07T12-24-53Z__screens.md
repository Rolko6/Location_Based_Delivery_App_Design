## Design Specificity Verdict

LLM assessment: clearly authored for this product. Cream + espresso + Shrikhand + rust is its own; the range ring, the 2.3 example photo with the "you" inset, the café-named privacy note and the BeReal-style dual camera belong only here. Cart (2.2) and checkout (2.7) are category-standard, which is fine for Operate; 2.9 Order sent is generic (check circle + receipt) at the flow's emotional peak.

Deterministic scan: not applicable (native platform).

## Overall Impression

The visual world is consistent and right. The bigger problem is the flow: five screens between "Go to checkout" and payment, and several competing ways of showing progress. Biggest opportunity: shorten the repeat customer's path (Mikko) and unify progress.

## What's Working

- 2.3 Confirm your spot: the example photo with the "you" inset and the café-named privacy note teach without text and answer Laura's "who sees me?".
- Range as a visual language: range ring, In range / Out of range group labels and the café split on 1.6 surface the product's key fact before reading.
- Thumb-zone primary actions: one bottom action per screen, big central shutter, QuickType code suggestion, Face ID confirm.

## Priority Issues

- **[P1] Too many steps for a repeat order.** Cart → 2.3 explainer → 2.4 selfie → 2.4b spot → 2.5 review → 2.7 Pay → 2.8 Face ID = 7 taps; 2.3 and 2.5 appear on every order. Conflicts with "Stay where you are: few steps" (Mikko, Laura). Fix: (a) show 2.3 only on the first order, later go from cart straight to the camera with a one-line privacy note in the camera; (b) one shutter press takes both photos (selfie, short countdown, back camera automatically), 2.4/2.4b become states of one screen; (c) skip 2.5 when the photo is sharp, keep "Retake" on the checkout thumbnail. Command: /impeccable distill.
- **[P2] Three progress systems.** Stepper on 2.3/2.7; "Step 2 of 3 · Photo" title plus "1 of 2" hint on 2.4/2.4b; "Step 3 of 3 · Pay" text on 2.8; nothing on 2.2 and 2.5. Fix: one stepper on every checkout screen incl. 2.2 and 2.5, compact cream variant on camera screens; title "Photo", sub-progress as two dots by the shutter. Command: /impeccable layout.
- **[P2] Copy that contradicts the product.** 1.2 "The café uses it to contact you" implies calls (product promises none); 1.5 "You can still look through café menus" has no path to menus; 2.7 "Deliver to: Saaripaahtimo · Your spot" reads as delivery to the café. Fix: rewrite 1.2 (code only / in-app messages), add "Browse cafés" link on 1.5, 2.7 "Your spot near Saaripaahtimo". Command: /impeccable clarify.
- **[P2] Meta lines wrap with a leading "·" and data disagrees.** "· Delivery €0.50", "· Out of delivery range" (1.6), "· Open until 18:00" (2.1); distance 45 m (1.6, 2.1, 2.2) vs 40 m (2.3, 2.8); "Delivery" vs "Delivery / service fee"; cart rows mix option vs price. Fix: two fixed meta lines without leading separators, one distance and one fee label across the flow, cart rows always "option · price". Command: /impeccable polish.
- **[P3] The purchase peak (2.9) is generic.** Check circle + receipt table at the peak-end moment. Fix: show the handoff instead ("Saaripaahtimo has your photo" with thumbnail, 4-step progress at Received, big 9:51); keep the receipt in 4.2. Command: /impeccable delight.

## Persona Red Flags

- **Mikko (repeat, between calls):** reads 2.3 every time, two photos on two screens, confirms 2.5; 7 taps cart → Face ID; no "Order again" yet; "Map" on 2.3 dead.
- **Laura (one hand, a few seconds):** back buttons top-left on 2.2/2.3/2.7 (swipe-back mitigates); 2.5's three-line note is too long for a two-second glance; the "1 of 2 · Your face" hint disappears on a dark shirt on 2.4.
- **Jordan (first-timer):** "Current GPS position ▾" is technical (1.6); two step counters on 2.4; "Deliver to Saaripaahtimo" read literally (2.7); can't find the promised menu browsing on 1.5.

## Minor Observations

- 1.3 "Resend code in 0:39" is underlined while inactive; underline only when tappable.
- 1.6 tab bar uses buttons without links; Orders tab dead in the prototype (4.1/2.10 already link).
- 2.1 → 2.2: milk is never chosen in the menu, yet the cart says "Oat milk".
- 2.4b: olive check on the selfie inset is small.
- 2.6: dimming around the QR frame is nearly invisible.
- 2.8: the screen behind the Apple Pay sheet is a "Pay" screen that doesn't exist in the flow; should be 2.7 Checkout.
- Camera "?" (2.4, 2.4b) has no destination.
- 2.10 is a strong end: the live card continues 2.9, so the end beats the peak.

## Questions to Consider

- What if one shutter press took both photos, BeReal-style?
- Does a customer need the 2.3 explainer on their tenth order?
- Does Mikko pay Shoot or the café? If the café stays in charge, should the sheet say "Pay Saaripaahtimo"?
- What if 2.9 weren't a receipt but the first frame of the handoff?
