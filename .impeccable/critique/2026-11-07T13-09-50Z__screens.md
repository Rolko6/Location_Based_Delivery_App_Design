## Design Specificity Verdict

LLM assessment: still unmistakably Shoot, now with an ending. 4.2b "Location sharing stopped / Your photo was deleted" turns "Privacy you can see" into a promise kept; no other delivery app has that moment. Empty stools as the example teach "spot" better than a crowd. The biggest new problem is a side effect of the added receipt on 4.2.

Deterministic scan: not applicable (native platform).

## Overall Impression

Laura's story closes and Aino can see what she ordered; the flow works from first launch to delivery. 4.2 paid for it: the receipt row took ~70 px from the map and the courier now sits under the "Location shared until delivery" tag. Biggest opportunity: lighten 4.2 so the map leads again.

## What's Working

- 4.2b Delivered fulfils C8 visibly: two olive confirmations in place of the map, "Eetu brought it · chat closed".
- Empty stools as the example photo teach to photograph the spot, not people.
- One stepper and unified data across ordering: 2.2 → 2.3 → camera → 2.5 → 2.7 → 2.8; 45 m everywhere; fee always "Delivery".

## Priority Issues

- **[P1] The courier on the 4.2 map is hidden under a tag.** The receipt shortened the map, the SVG slice moved, and the "E" marker now sits under "Location shared until delivery". Laura glances at 4.2 to see where the courier is. Fix: move the sharing tag to the top-right or next to "Your spot" at the bottom; place the route so the courier sits in the middle third at any map height; verify with the receipt collapsed and open. Command: /impeccable layout.
- **[P2] 4.2 is crowded.** Courier card wraps to four lines ("Eetu is / bringing it / Café staff · / has your photo"), receipt below, map a third smaller than two steps ago. Fix: courier card in two lines ("Eetu · café staff" / "Has your photo"); merge the receipt into the courier card as its bottom row instead of a separate card. Command: /impeccable layout.
- **[P2] Earlier orders are dead ends.** "Earlier" cards on 4.1/2.10 have no chevron or link although the receipt and Delivered view exist. Aino can't see what the team had; Mikko can't see what he ordered, the first step to "Order again". Fix: earlier cards open the 4.2b-style Delivered view with the receipt (without the live privacy confirmations); add a chevron like the live card. Command: /impeccable polish.
- **[P3] "Prepared" step label while preparing.** E8 says "Being prepared" while "Prepared" is lit as if done. Fix: "Preparing" (labels were approved from the wireframe in design-brief-final, so the user decides). Command: /impeccable clarify.

## Persona Red Flags

- **Laura:** can't see the courier on 4.2 (under the tag); 4.2b reassures her; example photo no longer nudges her to photograph other children; E9 "can't be refunded" still harsh.
- **Mikko:** repeat order skips 2.3, 6 taps cart → Face ID; "on time" on 4.2b confirms precision; can't open earlier orders; "Order again" missing (awaits wireframe).
- **Aino:** receipt lets her check "1× Cappuccino, oat milk · 2× Croissant"; can't find the team's past orders; group order still not hinted.

## Minor Observations

- 2.4 "First, a selfie" hint nearly vanishes on the placeholder's dark shirt.

- "Oat milk" in the cart still can't be chosen in the menu.
