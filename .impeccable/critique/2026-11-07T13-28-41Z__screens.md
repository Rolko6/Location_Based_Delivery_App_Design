## Design Specificity Verdict

LLM assessment: the new screens fit without changing the identity. The group speaks the product's language ("One delivery to your spot. Only you take the photo."), pickup leads with the order number rather than a map, E9b's map card shows the waiting courier with a timer. Weakness: the group ends in the solo checkout, so its story breaks halfway.

Deterministic scan: not applicable (native platform).

## Overall Impression

The app now covers every persona's whole path, incl. Aino's group and Mikko's repeat. The main problems are in how the new parts connect to the old: the group gets lost in the normal checkout, "Order again" is far from one tap, and the "Order again" card shows for someone who has never ordered.

## What's Working

- G2 Group cart: avatars with a dashed "Joona is choosing", items per person and "Who pays?" make a vague group order legible.
- 4.5 Pick up at the counter: the big "#2041" is exactly what the counter needs; "Location sharing stopped · You're picking it up yourself." keeps the privacy promise when plans change.
- E8, E9, E9b and 4.5 form a closed recovery system: every bad situation has at least two real exits and a stated refund rule.

## Priority Issues

- **[P1] The group order ends in the solo checkout.** G2 → 2.3 → 2.7 shows "Total €11.70" and solo items instead of the group's €11.80 and three people; "Everyone pays for their own" has no path, friends never pay; G3 "Add to Aino's order" drops Ville into Aino's cart (G2) although he is on his own phone. Fix: G4 group checkout (summary per person, chosen payment mode; split mode = host pays own share and waits) and G5 friend confirmation ("Added to Aino's order", who pays, "Pay your share €3.60" with Apple Pay when split). Command: /impeccable shape.
- **[P2] "Order again" is not one tap (C5).** Order again → cart → checkout → selfie → spot → review → Pay → Face ID = 8 taps. A photo is required per order, so true one-tap is impossible, but the cart is unnecessary for a repeat. Fix: "Order again" goes straight to the camera (2.4) with the old items; cart editable from checkout; label "Order again · €11.70". Command: /impeccable distill.
- **[P2] The host avatar breaks the system's own rule.** `.avatar.you` (G2, G3, design-system page) puts a white 13px letter on Caramel Orange: 3.85:1, below AA for small text and against "The Accent-Is-Not-Text Rule". Fix: espresso avatar with a cream letter (9.91:1), or keep sand with a caramel ring. Command: /impeccable polish.
- **[P2] "Order again" shows for someone who has never ordered.** 1.1 → 1.4 → 1.6 is a first launch, yet Home shows "Order again · Saaripaahtimo". Fix: show the card only after a first completed order (the prototype already remembers it from 2.9). Command: /impeccable harden.

## Persona Red Flags

- **Aino (group):** starts a group in 2 taps and sees who's missing; at payment sees a solo order and doesn't know if she pays for everyone; Ville ends up in her cart instead of his own confirmation.
- **Mikko (repeat):** "Order again" is the first thing on Home; 8 taps from it to Face ID and no price on the button.
- **Laura (privacy, one hand):** E9b and 4.5 give clear paths with privacy stated; right after sign-up she is confused by an "Order again" card for an order she never made.

## Minor Observations

- 4.5 back always goes to E8, even from E9; "Ready in ~6 min" is wrong when coming from E9 (already made).
- Cancel with full refund (Received) is only a written rule, no screen.
- 4.4 is sparse with two orders; "That's all your orders so far." handles it well.
- Home (1.6) got longer; with more cafés in range they fall below the first screen.
- Tapping Kampuskuppi / Ruska on 1.6 and the Profile tab still lead nowhere.

## Questions to Consider

- With "Everyone pays for their own", does Ville need the app at all, or is an Apple Pay payment link enough?
- Should "Order again" also skip the photo review (2.5) when the photo is sharp?
- What does Aino see if Joona never chooses: does the group wait, or go without him?
