# References

How to use this folder:
- **Layout and structure always come from my wireframes** (`wireframes/`). References never override them.
- `apps/` = real apps → inspiration for **how it feels** (clean, modern, simple), not what it looks like.
- `inspiration/` = Pinterest concepts → **visual mood** (style, shapes, illustrations).
- **Colors always come from `colorpallets/color_palette_1.png`**, never from references.

---

## apps/ – real apps (onboarding screens from Mobbin)

Relevant for screens: **1.1–1.6 (Sign-up)**

### Starbucks – main reference ⭐
- What I like: **simplicity and a modern feel.** Clean screens, plenty of white space, one clear action per screen, nothing extra.
- Take: the calm, uncluttered feeling, clear hierarchy (title → short text → one main button), short and focused copy.
- Don't take: their colors (green), their branding, their layouts.

### Bolt Food – main reference ⭐
- What I like: **the arrangement of elements.** How content is laid out on the screen, spacing, how the main button sits at the bottom within thumb reach.
- Take: spacing and rhythm, button placement, how fast and light the flow feels.
- Don't take: their colors (green), their branding. If their layout differs from my wireframe, **my wireframe wins**.

### Panera Bread – secondary reference
- Use only for comparison. Lower priority than Starbucks and Bolt Food.
- Don't take: colors, branding, layouts.

### In short
Make my wireframes feel as **simple as Starbucks** and as **well-arranged as Bolt Food**, in **our colors** (palette 1) and **our style** (see inspiration below).

## inspiration/ – Pinterest concepts (visual mood only)

### Main visual direction: insp-4, insp-5 (coffee app concept)
- Take: cream background + dark brown, rounded pill buttons and cards, chunky retro headline font, friendly mascot / illustrations inside cards, bottom tab bar with rounded icons, map + timeline on the tracking screen.
- Ignore: their features (AI mode, schedule your coffee, rewards), which are not in our app. Our layouts come from my wireframes.

### Illustrations and characters: insp-1, insp-2, insp-3
- Take: simple line-art characters (one stroke color, no shading), playful poses, people holding coffee, groups at a table (insp-3 fits group order).
- Use them in: onboarding, empty states (3.1 no café in range), success (2.9 order sent), errors (E1–E10).
- Colors: NOT their neon yellow / pink / blue. Use palette 1.
- In mockups, use simple placeholders. Final illustrations will come from a licensed pack or our own drawings.

### Overall vibe
Modern + retro, warm, friendly and playful, but the UI itself stays clean and readable (Inter).
