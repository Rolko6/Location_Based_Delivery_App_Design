Colour

Palette 1 carries the brand. Cream and brown come from palette 3 and are used only for background and text. Rust is the one colour that means “tap here”.

Rules of thumb

Do

  • One rust button per screen, docked at the bottom.
  • Pair every state colour with an icon and words.
  • Use the range ring wherever distance decides what you can do.
  • Keep tap targets at 44 px or more, with at least 8 px between them.
  • Leave room for Finnish (about 30% longer).

Don’t

  • Small text in orange or olive.
  • Shrikhand on buttons, prices or anything you read twice.
  • Moodboard patterns (stripes, dots, checkerboard), gradients, emoji.
  • Small labels above headings, or cards inside cards.
  • “Our couriers”. The courier belongs to the café.
